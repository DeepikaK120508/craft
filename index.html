Need it for a day? Borrow it from a classmate.

CRAFT (Campus Resource Sharing Platform) brings students together to request books, notes, calculators, lab equipment and more, to borrow or receive free.

Get Started View Requests

Why CRAFT?

Buy less

Need a calculator or lab coat for one exam? Ask a classmate before buying a new one.

Help in emergencies

A little help from a classmate can make a busy college day easier.

Borrow or receive free

Ask for a short-term loan or something a classmate is happy to give free.

Share on campus

Choose a campus collection point, such as the library, canteen or department entrance.

How It Works

Project in progress: the steps below describe the planned workflow. Request acceptance, My Sharing and notifications are still being completed and tested.
  1. Post a request. Log in and enter the item, description, campus collection location, needed time, deadline and emergency choice.
  2. A classmate accepts. Another signed-in student accepts an open request. The plan is to allow one helper, hide the accepted request from Requests and keep it saved.
  3. See the update. The requester will see the helper under My Requests. The helper will see it under My Sharing, and the requester will receive a notification.

Emergency requests

The planned Requests page will show all open requests automatically, with emergency requests first. No search or category filter will be needed. Emergency saving and ordering are still being completed and tested.
